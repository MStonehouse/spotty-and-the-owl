Great apparel starts with great artwork

Clean files give clean lines, accurate detail and consistent results. Here's what to send us, and why it matters.

Why vector artwork?

Vector graphics are built from shapes rather than pixels, so they can be scaled to a cap front or a full jacket back without losing sharpness. Pixel images blur and break up as they get bigger.

When you prepare your file

  • Convert fonts to outlines or curves
  • Include every element of the design
  • Embed or supply any linked images
  • Note Pantone colours if colours must match
  • Send a separate file for each design variation

Not every image is production-ready

Images taken from websites, screenshots, social media, messaging apps and AI tools often don't have the resolution or construction needed for a professional finish.

  • Small or pixelated JPEG files may print blurry.
  • AI-generated artwork can contain distorted lettering, irregular lines or stray background elements.
  • Upscaling a poor image doesn't restore missing detail.
  • Some methods need a transparent background.
  • AI-generated logos aren't automatically suitable for embroidery or screen printing.

Review and approval

We review all artwork before production. If a file isn't suitable, we'll ask for a replacement, recommend professional preparation, or quote to rebuild it for you. Production only begins once your artwork is approved.

Before you approve, please check the spelling and wording, logo accuracy, colours, proportions, placement and garment details. Changes after approval may add cost and time.
