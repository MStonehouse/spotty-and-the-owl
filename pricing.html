Order sizes

We work with orders of different sizes, depending on the method and production requirements.

  • Larger orders may qualify for volume pricing.
  • Screen printing may have minimum production quantities.
  • Embroidery may involve a one-time digitizing or setup fee.
  • Small orders may have minimum charges.
  • Individual names and numbers may add to the cost.

Bringing your own garments?

We may customize customer-supplied garments, subject to approval and inspection first. A handling surcharge may apply, and the price can be higher than on garments we source, because different fabrics react differently to heat, ink and stitching.

Any surcharge is shown in your quote before you approve the order.
