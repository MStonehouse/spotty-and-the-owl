More than apparel. A way to represent your brand.

At Spotty and the Owl Apparel, customizing apparel is more than a job. It's what we love doing.

We started the business in 2018, built on years of hands-on experience and a real love for well-made branded gear, to close a gap we kept seeing in custom apparel: a lack of professionalism, clear communication and reliable craftsmanship.

Apparel is never just something you wear. It's how you represent your business, build team spirit, promote an event and create a sense of pride and belonging. Our job is to help you wear what you really want.

Don't just say it. Wear it.

Straightforward from start to finish

From choosing the right blank garment and preparing your artwork to picking the best finish for your budget and timeline, we guide you through every step, so you get a finished product you're proud to wear.

Every project gets proper attention: clear expectations, honest recommendations and quality workmanship, from the first quote to the final count.
