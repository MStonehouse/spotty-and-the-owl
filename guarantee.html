Our guarantee and policies

We stand behind our workmanship. Here's exactly what that means, so there are no surprises.

Our production guarantee

Our goal is finished apparel that matches the approved artwork, garment selection, method and order details. Before an order is complete, we check it for noticeable production defects and count it against the approved quantity.

If an item has a confirmed manufacturing or finishing defect caused by our production process, contact us within 7 calendar days of receiving your order. We'll review the issue and, where the claim is justified, arrange a fair remedy, which may include:

  • Repair, where practical
  • Replacement of the affected item
  • Reprinting or re-embroidery
  • A refund or credit for the affected part of the order

Any remedy depends on the nature of the issue and applicable consumer protection laws.

What isn't covered

  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage from improper washing or drying
  • Damage from misuse or accidents
  • Pre-existing defects in customer-supplied garments
  • Shrinkage or fabric behaviour outside our control
  • Differences between on-screen colours and physical garment or ink colours
  • Minor variations that are part of printing, embroidery or garment processes
  • Artwork errors that were approved by the customer
  • Changes requested after production approval

Customer-supplied apparel

We may customize garments you supply, subject to approval and inspection first.

  1. Garments must be clean, unused, in good condition and suited to the chosen method.
  2. We may decline garments that are damaged, unsuitable, difficult to process or likely to give a poor result.
  3. A handling and processing surcharge may apply.
  4. Customizing your own garments may cost more than customizing ones we source.
  5. All garments are counted and inspected before production.
  6. Please supply a few extra garments where possible, in case of production issues.

Why is there an extra charge?

Your own garments bring extra handling, inspection, setup and production risk. The surcharge covers that, and it's shown in your quote before you approve the order.

What we can guarantee

We can't guarantee the performance, durability or suitability of the original garment, only our own workmanship in the customization we perform. Fabrics, finishes, seams and coatings all react differently to heat, pressure, ink and stitching. Please talk to us before buying garments to have customized.

Your order, your agreed quantity

Quantity matters, especially for uniforms, schools and teams.

  • Every approved order states the garment quantity.
  • Quantities are checked before production.
  • Finished orders are counted before pickup or delivery.
  • We tell you about any production issue that affects the agreed quantity.
  • We never knowingly substitute garments, sizes or colours without your approval.
  • Any shortfall or excess is resolved according to your approved quote.
